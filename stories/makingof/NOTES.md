## What is least sure

1. **Motion and sound were not watched.** Every chapter was read as frames at full size, on consecutive frames across
   every cut, and at phone size; cuts and entrances are measured against the music (marks). Nobody has watched it as
   video with the song: whether the edit breathes, whether the hole lands, whether chapter 9 feels like punchlines, only
   a person can say.
2. **Reading time is under the rule in many places** (check.mjs lists each). The worst: the centrepiece quote has 3.4 s
   for 13 words (the hole is 3 s long), the push-back 5.4 s for 19, the end credits 12.8 s for 47 words with the
   licences. Nothing is under 70% of the rule except the credits.
3. **Attribution calls that could go either way**: "headless blender?" is tagged HIS IDEA though it is a two-word
   question (quotes.md calls it a direction); "soo good, but oopsie" is tagged REJECTED; the shades are HIS IDEA, shown
   among the fixes; "tane needs to be a bit fatter" carries no tag and his pick of v3b carries HIS CALL; the two LEFT TO
   COOK entries are his good-night message and a caption ("Nine more sections. One agent each."), the second not a quote.
   The tally (8 · 4 · 9 · 7 · 5 · 2) counts only what this video shows.
4. **Chapter 6 runs backwards on the timeline** (the cast was designed at 20:11 to 20:36, before the puppet was rejected
   at 21:35): the script's order is by subject, and the clock at the bottom shows the true time of each item, so its
   marker jumps back.
5. **Stills from the finished film stand in for ideas** in chapter 6 (under his three answers) and chapter 8 (the map):
   each is labelled "in the finished film" or is the section being built.
6. **The seams** are shown at 1:1 on the front-facing frame the puppet's turn ends on, with a ring round the feet. They are small on a phone;
   his words carry it there.
7. **"NB2"** in his quote (Nano Banana 2, the image model) is left unexplained.
8. **Things shown as corresponding**, each read at full size after the owner's correction: the first test's mannequins
   and take B (in register, same cell, same facing: prepare.py lays each drawing over its mannequin at one scale for all
   eight); the method's layout and take 9 (the same size cell for cell: one wipes over the other) and its eight cut
   drawings in the same order; the bake-off's two clips (the same frame of the same shot); the boss before and after (the
   same forty frames of the film); the crowd and the first kick (the same frames of the slice, from the 23:46 assembly,
   which is before the polish he saw: both faults were in both); his five screenshots, each followed by the shot it was
   taken from. Not in register and not claimed to be: the stick mannequins of chapter 4 (re-rendered; the drawings
